3. Visitor statistics with GoatCounter

To see how often this page is visited and where visitors come from, I use GoatCounter (operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland; servers at Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland). The home page loads a script from gc.zgo.at for this. It processes the page visited, the referrer (the page you came from), browser and operating system, screen width, language setting and the country derived from your IP address. It also counts clicks on links to other websites and on the email link (only the link target, no content).

GoatCounter stores neither your IP address nor your full browser identifier, and sets no cookies or other storage in your browser. To recognise repeat views, your IP address and browser identifier are mapped to a random ID in memory only, for at most 8 hours, and then discarded. I only see aggregated numbers, never individual people.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is knowing the reach of my online CV. You can object by blocking scripts from gc.zgo.at in your browser (e.g. with a content blocker). Details: GoatCounter's privacy notice.

Apart from this, the website embeds no cookies, advertising or tracking services, external fonts or other third-party content.

4. Local storage in your browser

If you change the language, colour scheme (light/dark) or sort order, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it is kept for your next visit. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to me or anyone else. Storing it is strictly necessary for the function you explicitly requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.
